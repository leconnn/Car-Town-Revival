Car Town Revival is a free, unofficial fan game. This policy explains what we store when you play **online**. Single-player and LAN play don't send anything to us.

## What we collect

- **Your email address**, to sign you in and to let you reset or confirm your account.
- **A display name** you choose. Please don't use your real name.
- **Your game progress** (your garage, cars, coins, missions and so on).

We don't collect real names, birthdays, photos, contacts, location or payment details. There are no ads and no trackers, and we never sell or share your data.
