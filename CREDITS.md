# Credits

**Car Town Revival** is an unofficial, free fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Electronic Arts, Glu Mobile or Cie Games. It will never be sold, and it doesn't accept donations.

## Car Town

Car Town was created by **Cie Games** (2010). The Car Town name, art, sounds and data belong to **Electronic Arts** (via Glu Mobile / Cie Games). This remake uses them for a non-commercial fan project and asks nothing in return.
